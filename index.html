Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take on freelance projects?
Yes! I am always open to discussing new freelance opportunities, collaborations, and contract work. Feel free to reach out via the contact form.
What is your primary tech stack?
My primary backend stack revolves around PHP (Laravel & CodeIgniter) and Node.js. For the frontend, I specialize in React.js, JavaScript, and Bootstrap/Tailwind CSS. Additionally, I have extensive experience building full-stack web applications using the complete MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js).
Can you redesign or update my existing website?
Yes, I frequently help clients redesign their outdated websites. I focus on improving the user experience (UI/UX), making the site fully mobile-responsive, and optimizing performance by migrating to modern frameworks.
Do you provide ongoing website maintenance?
Absolutely. I offer maintenance packages to ensure your website remains updated, secure, and fully optimized after launch.
How long does it take to build a website?
Timelines depend on project complexity. A standard corporate website typically takes 2-4 weeks, while custom e-commerce platforms can take 4-8 weeks.
Do you handle hosting and domain setup?
Yes, I can assist with end-to-end deployment. This includes server setup (cPanel, AWS, VPS), SSL certificate installation, and connecting your domain so your website is completely ready to go live.
